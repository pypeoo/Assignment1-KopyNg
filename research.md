My goals focus on my personal life and future lifestyle, unlike the academic goals in the article I read, which are about developing competence in a professional program. My personal goals are self-directed and measured by my own sense of balance and fulfillment, whereas the article's goals are assessed and tied to professional competence. Still, both articulate my personal definition of "success".
