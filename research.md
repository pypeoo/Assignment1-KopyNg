# **Research here**
Over the next five years, I hope to live a happy and balanced life. I want to take care of my lovely pets, spend more quality time with my family, run my own cafe, and enjoy outdoor activities like hiking and camping. My goals focus on my personal life and future lifestyle, unlike the academic goals examined by _Oudkerk Pool et al. (2020)_, which are about developing competence in a professional program. However, the two are connected because both require learning, improvement, responsibility, and reflection. To make it sound more realistic, becoming a UX designer and running my own café will both require creativity, problem-solving, communication, and planning. My goals are self-directed and evaluated by my own sense of balance and fulfillment, whereas competency-based goals are externally assessed against defined professional standards. 
_Oudkerk Pool et al. (2020)_ found that competency-based portfolios provide an accurate but fragmented picture of student development and that students would benefit from coaching on selecting meaningful experiences to document. This is relevant to my academic goals because I want my UX portfolio to show off not only the projects I have completed, but also the story of my growth and what I learned from each one. I find this research interesting and relevant to my career and educational goals. For me, being a UX designer means staying attuned to the user or client, plus creativity to address user needs. Still, both articulate my personal definition of "success".
